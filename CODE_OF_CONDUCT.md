# Code of Conduct

Feitio follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
everyone who takes part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) does so with
respect, whatever their experience, identity or background.

In short:

- Be kind and assume good faith. Criticize ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing anyone's private
  information.
- Welcome newcomers; explain instead of dismissing.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@HenriqueArtur](https://github.com/HenriqueArtur), through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block anyone who does not
follow this code, as the Contributor Covenant describes.
