# Guidelines

How Feitio looks, moves and speaks, and why. Each page ends with what to avoid.

- [Voice](voice.md): the idea, the values, the three things Feitio never is, and how text is written.
- [Color](color.md): the six roles, the house theme by day and at night, status colors, themes for
  outside work.
- [Typography](typography.md): the Alegreya family and the type scale.
- [Space and density](space-and-density.md): one ruler for every gap; comfortable reading,
  compact work screens.
- [Shape and depth](shape-and-depth.md): uneven corners, the ink line, lifted paper.
- [Motion](motion.md): paper being placed, and no change of state that jumps.
- [Iconography](iconography.md): icons and illustrations drawn by hand.

These pages came out of sixteen drafts, one decision at a time. The whole system was chosen by
looking at it, not described in the abstract first.
