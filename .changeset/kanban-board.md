Kanban and KanbanCard: work in columns, one per stage. Wide, a file of folders with tabs and cards held by tape; on a phone, stage tabs. Cards move by dragging (they follow the hand on a spring) or with their button (phone and keyboard), only to the columns they list; every move sends the cancelable `feitio-kanban-move` first. Columns may offer a new-card button (`feitio-kanban-create`). The sides fade while there is more to scroll, and a map of the board shows when the columns do not fit. The card's content is free.
