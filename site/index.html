Feitio

Made by hand, full of energy.

A design system for interfaces that show the care of whoever made them and the will to keep going. Feitio is Portuguese for the way a thing is made.

Shape, depth and motion

Uneven corners, like paper cut by hand. What floats is lifted paper, with a fine ink line and a hard shadow, and it arrives like a cut-out laid on the table. Every change of state is a transition.
